About me

I'm Yassy — a student who got hooked on technology the moment I realized I could build things instead of just using them. What started as curiosity about how websites work turned into a habit of taking things apart, breaking them, and figuring out how to make them work again — better than before.

I don't learn well from watching alone. I learn by opening the console, writing the wrong code first, reading the error, and trying again. That loop — build, break, fix, understand — is how I actually pick things up.
